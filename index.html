How it works

Fill or refund

The draw only happens when every spot is sold. If a raffle isn't full by its deadline, everyone gets their SOL back.

Holders only

Hold 2M of the coin for 24 hours to buy spots. Max 2 spots per wallet per raffle.

A free spot for a holder

When a raffle fills, one extra spot goes to a random holder, for free. Coin fees pay for these extras.

Provably fair

The spots are locked first, then the hash of a Solana block about 15 seconds later picks the winner. Anyone can check it.

Real prizes

Prizes are bought on Courtyard after the raffle fills. You get the item there: keep it, ship it home, or sell it back.

Admin

18+ only. Raffles aren't available where they're prohibited, so check your local rules before you play. Payments are in SOL to the treasury wallet; prizes are bought on Courtyard after a raffle fills. Nothing here is financial advice.
